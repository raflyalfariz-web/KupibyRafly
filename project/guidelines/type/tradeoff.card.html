A handwriting font would match the logo and hurt the prices. Faces like Caveat or Kalam fall apart at 13px on a cheap Android screen, and prices are the thing people squint at. Zilla Slab keeps the warmth in the serifs and stays sharp at 11px. Cost: the wordmark stays visibly hand-made and the type does not echo it.

Both faces are on Google Fonts and loaded from the CDN. No self-hosted binaries to maintain. Cost: an offline device falls back to Georgia and Arial — the layout survives, the character does not.
