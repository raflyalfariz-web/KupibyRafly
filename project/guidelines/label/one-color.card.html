Why it holds up

  • Every line is 3px / ~0.8mm or heavier. No hairlines to close up in the screen.
  • No reversed type at all — brown ink sits on cream stock, so nothing depends on register.
  • Flat solids only. No gradients, no halftone, no drop shadows.
  • Type floor on the label is 11px / ~3mm at 700 weight; the batch box is written by hand in pen.
  • Condensation test: nothing smaller than the WA number, and the number is the last thing anyone needs wet.

Trade-off: a 1-colour label means the size is a brown outlined box, not a bright block — slightly harder to tell 500ml from 1L across a cooler at a glance. Sticker stock colour is doing the work the second ink would do.
