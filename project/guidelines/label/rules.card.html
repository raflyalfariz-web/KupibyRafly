Give the vendor

  • One PNG per ink, 300dpi, at final size, black on transparent.
  • Label sizes: 500ml = 60 × 95 mm. 1L = 78 × 122 mm.
  • Ink: brown Pantone 476 C (nearest to #44250E). Amber: Pantone 143 C.
  • Stock: cream vinyl or kraft, matte, waterproof adhesive.

Non-negotiable

  • No gradient, no halftone, no tint percentages — solids only.
  • Minimum line 0.8 mm. Minimum type 3 mm at 700 weight.
  • No reversed type under 4 mm. Big reversed only, or none.
  • 3 mm bleed, 4 mm safe margin from every trimmed edge.
  • Rectangle with rounded corners. No die-cut shapes.

Wet test before you approve a run: spray a printed label, leave it 10 minutes in a fridge, then read it at arm’s length. If the batch box or the WA number goes soft, the type is too small or the ink too thin.
