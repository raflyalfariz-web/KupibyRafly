Rules for the second ink

  • Amber (#E9A63C) does exactly one job: the size block. Nothing else changes.
  • Brown always prints over amber, with a 3px brown keyline around the block so a 1mm mis-register never shows as a white gap.
  • Never amber type on cream at label sizes — too little contrast when wet.
  • Amber never touches the roof mark or the wordmark. Those stay 100% brown.

Trade-off: two screens roughly doubles setup cost per run and adds a register risk with a small vendor. Worth it only once you are printing both sizes in the same batch.
