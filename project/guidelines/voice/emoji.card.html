Boleh

  • 🙏 after a thank-you or an apology. 👇 above a form. That is the whole list.
  • One exclamation mark per message, maximum.
  • Line breaks instead of commas when listing.
  • Lowercase “ya”, “kok”, “kan” — write how Rafly talks.

Jangan

  • Emoji inside a price, a total or a batch code. Ever.
  • 🔥☕✨ strings, ALL CAPS words, “buruan”, “dijamin”, “terbaik”.
  • “Kak” to a neighbour you know the name of — use the name.
  • Formal Indonesian (“dipersilakan”, “mohon ditunggu”). It reads like a bank.
