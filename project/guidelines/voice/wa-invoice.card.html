Structure rules

  • Header line: name + day + date. No “INVOICE #”.
  • One item per line, price right-ish; an em-dash rule above the total is the only ornament.
  • Total is the last number. Nothing after it except how to pay.
  • Payment in one line, two options, no instructions.
  • Never ask for a screenshot. Confirm receipt yourself.

Trade-off: aligned columns break on some fonts and narrow screens. Two spaces minimum between label and price; accept slight raggedness rather than tabs.
