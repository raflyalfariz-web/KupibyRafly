Structure rules

  • The form is one message, never split — people copy the whole block.
  • Labels are one word, aligned by monospace-ish padding so the reply is scannable.
  • Fields in the order Rafly works: who, where, when, what, how sweet.
  • Dots for blanks, not brackets — brackets get left in.
  • Confirm with the total, then promise the detail. Never both in one message.

Trade-off: a form in a chat bubble is uglier than a web form, and it works on every phone with no link to trust. Keep it.
